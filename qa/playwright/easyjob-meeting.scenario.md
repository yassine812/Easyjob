# EasyJob Meeting scenario

1. Sign in with a test recruiter.
2. Open a scheduled EasyJob Meeting.
3. Confirm that the consent dialog explains transcription and that declining keeps the meeting usable.
4. With consent, start transcription and verify the short caption/control state.
5. Stop the transcription worker or disable the model and verify the meeting and manual notes continue.
6. Finish the meeting and verify recruiter-only notes and the summary remain hidden from the candidate.
