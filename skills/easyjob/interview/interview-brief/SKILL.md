# interview-brief

- **Version:** 1.0.0
- **Purpose:** Prepare 8–12 explainable interview questions from exact job and CV snapshots.
- **Outputs:** question text, priority, skill, reason, evidence source, gap type.
- **Rules:** use human-readable reasons; do not expose raw internal status labels in the UI.
- **Fallback:** use the deterministic interview question bank.
