Modifications: only the occupation codes listed in scripts/import_job_knowledge.py
are retained. Field values in onet-31.0.json are otherwise unchanged. Download URLs
and SHA-256 hashes are stored in the snapshot. The source categories are broader
than the EasyJob specialties: e.g. Django is an EasyJob specialization of Software
Developers, not a separate O*NET occupation. Product Manager uses Marketing Managers
as a broad source category; the product-specific responsibilities are EasyJob edits.

roles.json and families.json are curated EasyJob editorial adaptations. French and
Arabic phrasing, seniority profiles, technologies and skill importance are EasyJob
editorial decisions, not official O*NET translations, ratings or endorsements.
O*NET task IDs and knowledge areas remain available in the generated provenance.
O*NET does not endorse EasyJob or the generated content.

No ESCO extract is currently bundled. No external dataset is downloaded at runtime.
